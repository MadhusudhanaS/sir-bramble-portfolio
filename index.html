Character sheet · 02

The guild’s smallest adventurer

Once the first face visitors saw at the Adventurers’ Guild, Sir Bramble is now the first to step forward when danger reaches Asterfall. Small, freckle-cheeked and a little clumsy—yet surprisingly good at facing monsters.

Name
Sir Bramble of Asterfall
What they are
A small, round-faced kid with big brown eyes, rosy cheeks and messy chestnut hair that never lies flat. Star headband, rust-orange tunic, green cape, the old guild desk bell at his collar, a pocket lantern and a star-tipped wand. Polite, nervous and secretly fearless. Polite, nervous and secretly fearless. Bramble greets every stranger with “Welcome to the guild!”, apologises to doors he bumps into, and is braver than he knows.
What they do
Adventurer of the Starfall Guild
Their story
Guild receptionist turned adventurer after a band of odd, ugly creatures invaded his city and the guild hall. When one cornered his friends, Bramble fought back—and discovered he had a real talent for monster-slaying.
Tagline
“Small cloak. Big courage. One very odd quest.”

Quests completed:

Credits: Sir Bramble and the pixel scenes are drawn in code for this portfolio. Sound is made live with the Web Audio API: Bramble’s babbles and giggles are synthesized in the browser, with no recordings.

Behind the build: Bramble began as a tiny guild receptionist who welcomed every traveller. The idea grew into a little pixel-art world where a shy, kind-hearted character discovers that courage means protecting the people who trust him. The page was built to feel like an early-web character portfolio: part storybook, part toy box, and full of small secrets.
